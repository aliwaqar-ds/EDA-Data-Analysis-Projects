## 🏏 Project 1: IPL 2022 Season Analysis
* **Objective:** Analyze 74 match records to uncover match outcome drivers, toss impact, top-performing players, and stadium trends.
* **Key Findings:**
  * Toss winning teams only won match outcomes **48.65%** of the time.
  * **Kuldeep Yadav** secured the most Player of the Match awards (4 times).
  * **Quinton de Kock** recorded the highest individual innings (140 runs), and **Jasprit Bumrah** delivered the best bowling figures (5/10).
  * Wankhede Stadium and Dr. DY Patil Sports Academy hosted the highest volume of matches.

## 🛒 Project 2: E-Commerce Customer & Revenue Analysis
* **Objective:** Evaluate order amounts, profit margins, delivery timelines, and customer review distributions across membership tiers.
* **Key Findings:**
  * **Electronics** and **Home & Kitchen** generated the largest overall profit volume.
  * Platinum tier members showed higher average review satisfaction compared to Standard tier accounts.

## 🏬 Project 3: Superstore Sales & Logistics EDA
* **Objective:** Identify top revenue-generating categories, regional demand distributions, and shipping latency by shipping tier.
* **Key Findings:**
  * **Technology** and **Furniture** led total category sales.
  * Same-Day and First-Class deliveries maintained average fulfillment within 0 to 2 days, while Standard delivery averaged ~5 days.
  * West and East regions drove the highest sales volume across core product lines.
